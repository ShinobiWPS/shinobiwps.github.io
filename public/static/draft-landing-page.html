High-impact client work

01 European blockchain platform Sustainability agency

The challenge

A European platform needed to track company environmental data and support transactions on a blockchain. The problem started before any transaction: there was no reliable way to enter and check company information. One wrong entry could then affect the rest of the process.

How I helped

I identified the weak point in how company data entered the platform and worked on making validation consistent throughout the process. From that foundation, I developed the interface for the whole platform, so company information could move between steps without losing its integrity.

02 Real-time editing platform Translation SaaS startup

The challenge

Redokun already had a working document-translation product, but its interface was outdated and not designed for the experience people expect today. Teams needed to edit the same document, see one another's changes and keep translations consistent. Think Google Docs, but with automatic translation and legal validity.

My contribution

I led the new interface, coordinating the important choices with the CTO and CEO. Instead of recreating old screens one by one, I focused on the journey from automatic translation to team review and approval. I also supported a junior developer working on the new frontend.

Outcome

The new interface was built separately from the existing translation service. That let the product move toward a smoother collaborative experience without replacing the system that already handled translations.

03 AI-assisted due diligence (AI integration) Energy SaaS startup

The challenge

Renow operates in renewable energy, where technical documents, legal requirements and administrative records all need careful review. These checks cover different questions, even when they belong to the same project. The work focused on using AI to support this review process.

My contribution

I implemented AI agents for technical, legal and administrative due diligence inside the product. The aim was to support those specific reviews, rather than build a generic AI feature. This AI integration brought specialized AI agents into the renewable-energy product to support those reviews.

Outcome

Users found the different quote-analysis options easier to understand and navigate, with less confusion. Conversion also increased.

04 Nation-wide Legal SaaS (AI integration) Enterprise legal SaaS

The challenge

TeamSystem was modernizing a widely used legal software product one section at a time. Some areas already had a new interface, while others still used the old one. People working with documents, calendars and daily office tasks sometimes had to switch between versions themselves.

My contribution

I worked on making the software choose the appropriate version automatically depending on the section being opened. There was an important exception: when someone deliberately chose the older interface, the product needed to remember that choice rather than override it. Another part of the work was an AI integration for the dashboard: a chatbot designed to let users find information and carry out operations through conversation instead of navigating the usual screens.

Outcome

This made the transition easier to navigate for existing users while the product continued to change gradually. They could use the newer sections without repeatedly managing the switch, and still keep control when they preferred the old version.
